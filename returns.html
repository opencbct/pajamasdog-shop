Shipping & Returns

By placing an order you agree to this policy.

Last updated: November 26, 2026. This page covers items sold by PajamasDog (marked "Sold by PajamasDog"). Items from partner shops such as Amazon follow that shop's own return policy.

In short — all sales are final
  • Changed your mind? Cancel within 12 hours of ordering for a full refund, before it ships.
  • No returns, refunds or exchanges for size or change of mind after that. Please measure your pet before you order.
  • Damaged, defective or wrong item: e-mail photos within 48 hours of delivery. We send a replacement.
  • No tracking movement 45 days after shipping: we ship it again.
  • We do not take items back. Please don't mail anything to us — a problem item is fixed by a free replacement shipped from our partner warehouse.

Shipping

Free shipping. Orders leave our partner warehouse in 1–3 days. We ship to the United States and Canada. Usual delivery: United States 7–12 days; Canada 7–15 days. We e-mail the tracking number when it ships. Import duties or taxes charged by your country, if any, are paid by the buyer.

Cancelling an order

You can cancel within 12 hours of ordering, before your order leaves the warehouse, for a full refund. Use the Order help page (or the link in your confirmation e-mail). The refund goes back to your card through Stripe. After 12 hours, or once the order has been sent to the warehouse, it can no longer be cancelled.

Damaged, defective or wrong item

E-mail us within 48 hours of delivery (per tracking) with your order e-mail and clear photos of: the problem, the product tag, and the shipping label. Claims after 48 hours, or without photos, cannot be accepted. If we confirm the problem we send a replacement of the same item; if it is no longer available we refund the item price. Minor differences in color or print placement, small loose threads, or packaging creases are not defects. One claim per order.

Size and fit

We do not offer returns, refunds or exchanges for size. Every pet is a different shape, so please measure your pet (see below) and check the sizes before ordering. If you are unsure, e-mail us your pet's measurements first and we'll suggest a size.

Ordered the wrong size? Within 14 days of delivery we can give you a 15% off code to order the right size. The first item is yours to keep; no refund is given for it.

Change of mind, worn or washed items

All sales are final. Items ship to you directly from our overseas partner warehouse, so we cannot accept returns for a change of mind. Items that were worn, washed, chewed or altered are not eligible for any claim.

No returns by mail. We do not have a returns address and cannot accept items sent back. Packages mailed to us or to our partner warehouse without our written instructions are refused and not refunded. If an item is damaged, defective or wrong, follow the steps above and we ship a replacement at no cost to you — you do not need to send the item back.

Lost or late orders

If tracking shows no movement 45 days after shipping, e-mail us and we will ship the item again once, or refund you if we cannot ship it. Orders shown as delivered by the carrier are the carrier's responsibility; please file a claim with the carrier. Please check that your shipping address is correct at checkout — we cannot reship or refund orders sent to an address you entered incorrectly.

Refunds

When a refund is approved, it goes back to your original payment method through Stripe and usually shows in 5–10 business days. Shipping fees and the exchange discount are not refundable.

Please contact us before a chargeback

We answer every e-mail within 2 business days. If you open a bank dispute without contacting us, we will send the bank your order details, delivery tracking and this policy, which you agreed to when you placed your order.

AI try-on is a preview

Our AI pictures show how a style looks on your pet. They are not a fit guarantee. Please choose the size from your pet's measurements.
