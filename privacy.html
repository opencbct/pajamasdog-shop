Privacy

Last updated: November 23, 2026. PajamasDog (pajamasdog.com) is operated by OPENCBCT LLC.

What we collect

We do not ask you to create an account. When you buy a PajamasDog product, payment is handled by Stripe (we never see your card), and we receive your name, e-mail and shipping address only to ship your order through our fulfilment partner. Purchases on partner shops (such as Amazon) happen under their own privacy policies.

Your dog's photo

On the try-on page, your photo is resized in your own browser. When you tap "Dress up with AI", the photo is sent to our AI partner, Runway, only to create your picture. We do not store it, and we never sell or publish it.

Partner links and cookies

Product links go to partner shops (such as Amazon, Coupang and NAVER) and affiliate networks (such as CJ Affiliate). When you click one, the partner may set cookies to record that the visit came from us, so we can earn a commission at no extra cost to you. As an Amazon Associate we earn from qualifying purchases.

  • CJ Affiliate: cj.com/legal/privacy-policy-services
  • Amazon: Amazon Privacy Notice

Region

We guess your region from your browser's time zone and language to show shops that ship to you. Nothing is stored or sent.
